Projects

Quadrotor hover stabilization

Python, NumPy, SciPy · team of 3 · In progress

An optimal (LQR) controller that keeps a quadrotor, a nonlinear and underactuated robot, steady in a hover. We linearize the dynamics around hover and solve the continuous-time algebraic Riccati equation two ways, with Schur decomposition and the matrix sign function, then test recovery from disturbances and simulated wind.

Tradbot-Engine: limit order book

C++17

A matching engine with price-time priority, partial fills, and iceberg orders that refill from hidden reserve and move to the back of the queue. Benchmarked at about 200 ns median and 2.7 µs p99 latency across 100,000 randomized orders. Next up: a custom memory pool to cut worst-case spikes.
